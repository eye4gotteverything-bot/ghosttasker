# GhosŧŦasker — Privacy Note

Plain-language summary of how this version of GhosŧŦasker (`ghosttasker`) handles your information.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter in this web version — groups and the phone numbers in them, message templates, weekly automations, sent history, your linked-app choice, and your plan selection — is stored **only in your own browser**, in `localStorage`, on your own device.

There is **no server** for this version. Nothing you enter is uploaded, synced, sold, or shared by the app, and there is no tracking or analytics.

## What the AI Bot uses

The AI Bot is a local helper that runs in your browser. It suggests drafts using only your own templates and sent history already stored in that browser. It does not call an external AI service, needs no API key, and sends nothing anywhere.

## Linked-app handoff

Sending is off until you select one specific app in the Apps tab and tick the approval box. When you tap send in the Send Queue, the app opens your linked app (for SMS / Messages, an `sms:` link) with the recipients and message filled in, and **you** tap send in that app. What happens after that handoff is handled by the app you linked, under that app's own privacy policy.

GhosŧŦasker does not send messages in the background and does not auto-send.

## Subscriptions

This web version displays plan pricing only (Basic free; Premium with a 3-day free trial, then $5.99/week, $19.99/month, or $149.99/year). It does not collect card details and does not start a subscription. On Android, Premium subscriptions run through Google Play Billing and are managed/cancelled in Google Play.

## Deleting your data and unlinking

- **Unlink:** in the Apps tab, tap "Unlink app (turn sending off)". That clears your linked-app choice and turns sending off.
- **Delete everything:** use the "Delete all my GhosŧŦasker data" button in the footer, or clear this site's data in your browser settings. That permanently removes the groups, numbers, templates, automations and history stored in that browser.
- Because data is stored per browser, using a different browser or device starts empty — nothing follows you there.

## Consent

Only message people who have agreed to receive your texts, and give them a way to opt out. Remove anyone who opts out from your groups.
